# Construal Plurality, Accumulation, And Corpus Design

Research audit, 2026-09-08. No new model inference, fitting, GPU work or payload
conversion in this pass. Sources: exact synthetic stimuli; the 24-prefix archive
survey; read-only lossless-agent archive navigation and opencode recall; local
Qwen3.8 fitting reports, saved validation and importer code; cited literature.

## What The Previous Panel Does Not Test

The [crossed-task panel](LENS-CROSSED-TASK.md) has 24 conditions over four worlds,
not 24 independent situations. Under its explicitly closed deterministic rules,
all four worlds select H1 and contradict H2. Thus **0/4 worlds retain multiple
causal explanations**. Both inferential jobs and the rule selecting one are also
explicit. Multiple supporting observations, possible checks and response phrasings
remain, but these are not competing interpretations of the causal situation.

The chain `letter -> codebook -> definition slot -> inferential relation` can
be incompletely represented or incorrectly executed. That is different from
maintaining several plausible meanings, goals or world hypotheses across turns.
An erroneous H2 prediction is not a newly licensed fictional hypothesis.

This was useful instrumentation and instruction-binding research. It is not a
representative assay of open-ended construal, relational calibration, or accumulated
interpretive loss. Replacing natural uncertainty with explicit rules improved
experimental control partly by deleting the phenomenon now under discussion.

## How Much Plurality Is In The Archive?

The original selection contains 24 distinct prefixes: 17 standalone
recontextualizations and seven ancestry-checked short histories. These are
purposively selected cases, not a prevalence sample.

Two assistant audits disagree because their inclusion rules differ:

| Screen | Result | Meaning and limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Broad working-construal tags | 21/24 flagged; 20 with multiple tags | Includes missing facts, goals, causal uncertainty, ontology and relational framing; not a validated ambiguity count |
| Two text-licensed readings with different substantive consequences | Six stronger candidates, six disputed | A narrower provisional inclusion range, not a confidence interval or an adjudicated truth set |
| Verified multi-turn exposure | Seven short histories | Provenance of a stack, not evidence that all seven accumulate loss |

The narrower screen identifies TypeScript type/runtime transformation,
Technicolor process/appearance, shoobie etymology/cartoon characterization,
emoji spatial interpretation, Homer/adaptation, and bundles/sets as stronger
candidates. It treats radiator, roast, outlet, hiking and equipment questions
primarily as missing facts or preferences rather than linguistic ambiguity.
The broad screen admits those as potentially consequential situation-model
uncertainty. Neither definition should silently substitute for the other.

In particular, missing facts are not merely unwanted noise if the target is the
model's working understanding of a situation. Conversely, every unspecified
detail or possible writing style should not count as another construal. Tags
are correlated; seven tag categories do not establish seven latent dimensions.
Annotation disagreement is itself a corpus-design result to retain.

## Distinctions To Preserve

- **Utterance interpretation:** which referent, construction, scope or meaning
  the words support; e.g. displaying a set on a sphere versus attaching a copy
  over each point of a base space.
- **World uncertainty:** what happened or which mechanism applies, even when
  the question is linguistically clear.
- **Pragmatic uncertainty:** whether the user seeks explanation, reassurance,
  criticism, implementation, or exploration; these can coexist.
- **Goal formation:** preferences may be negotiated or discovered during the
  exchange, not simply recovered from a hidden fixed intent.
- **Epistemic status:** who supplied a proposition, whether it is hypothetical,
  inferred, accepted, disputed, or independently evidenced.
- **Operational commitment:** an answer may mention several options yet plan,
  summarize or act as if only one remains. Verbal coverage is not preservation
  of alternatives for subsequent use.

Do not require every construal to be an exclusive label. A process-level and an
appearance-level account of Technicolor can both be relevant. Conversely, a
factual error does not become valid merely because someone can imagine it.

## Accumulation Can Change Status Without Losing Text

The user's earlier frame explicitly challenged the assumption that the canonical
trajectory fully specifies the underlying situation: intentions, constraints,
background and even the user's understanding can be incomplete. That prior
position is recovered in recall session `ses_feada9d36ffevFGRexXnuPwpix`, message
`msg_024e925e30019S05ptX1zTwue9`; this is lineage, not a newly invented program.

Read-only archive searches recovered several relevant patterns:

- An assistant promotes a mentioned opportunity into the central priority; after
  correction it overcorrects from "not central" to "rejected." Source text is
  still present. This is unsupported commitment, not demonstrated forgetting.
- A terse correction identifying the intended research topic is followed by a
  confident new mechanistic thesis. Recovery of the user's topic and creation
  of new assistant claims happen in the same turn.
- A relational interpretation enters the proposed framing of a research
  document. Some caveats remain: this is mixed calibration, not a clean case
  where every hypothesis becomes fact.
- A constructive architectural conversation narrows after an explicit user
  preference. This is a productive convergence control, not automatically drift.

The potential chain is:

```
partial user expression
  -> assistant construal
  -> paraphrase / recommendation / proposed memory
  -> user uptake, redirection, or silence
  -> a premise for later interpretation
```

An assistant's assertion is evidence that it asserted something, not automatically
evidence about the world or the user's commitments. Agreement may acknowledge
a useful direction without endorsing every embedded claim. Repetition can change
salience or perceived authority without adding independent support.

Separate deletion, provenance loss, confidence inflation, goal revision and
changed operational use. Autoregressive sequencing does not by itself imply
irreversible loss while original context remains accessible. Path dependence
after clarification need not be an attractor or an error: earlier turns can
legitimately create preferences, promises and shared vocabulary.

## Better Next Corpus: Small Casebook Before Another Batch

Prepare six to eight cases, not an expansive benchmark: a few genuinely forkable
semantic histories, open-world factual/goal uncertainty, and legitimate narrowing
controls. Recover ancestry before calling a standalone export a historical stack.
Selection from corrections biases toward failures; include successful interaction.

Each case needs an explicit instrument record:

1. Exact supplied history and known omissions, including missing retrieval,
   attachments, system context or memories; replay is not restoration of the
   original provider's complete internal state.
2. Plausible readings with textual support, counterevidence, incompatible and
   compatible combinations, and consequentially different downstream uses.
3. Separate ledgers for observations, source-attributed hypotheses, assumptions,
   user endorsements, newly formed preferences and assistant additions.
4. Witness continuations that distinguish readings, clearly marked **authored
   counterfactuals**, never recovered historical intent or retroactive gold.
5. A declared annotation authority and disagreements: linguistic licensing,
   domain facts, user intent and acceptable action cost have different oracles.
6. Success criteria that allow clarification, a conditional answer, a reversible
   default or a useful synthesis. "Always ask" and "mention every possibility"
   are not universal targets.

Promising seeds include uncertainty about which set/bundle construction is proposed;
an etymology correction that may instead concern a cartoon depiction; and an
industrial reproduction question whose success criterion may be perceptual.
Each needs domain checking and a consequence that distinguishes its readings.
New user evidence can resolve a desired target without making a technical claim
true. The Current offers a parallel distinction: authorized fictional world
creation versus unsupported attribution of player knowledge, motive or preference.

For a first controlled fork, compare source-attributed tentative interpretation,
the same hypotheses without attribution, prematurely committed interpretation,
genuine user-endorsed narrowing, and selective omission. These are not all equal-
information arms: omission and endorsement deliberately change what is known.
An attribution instruction can itself improve review behavior; include an
instruction-only control before attributing an effect specifically to provenance.
Attribution removal may also alter hedging, perceived authority, length or
repetition; source attribution and degree of commitment need separate contrasts.

Follow with the same appropriately scoped clarification and several prospective
uses: recommendation, recap, proposed memory, next question, or document revision.
Test repair and warranted reconvergence, not identical text. Distinguish paths
that differ only in framing from paths that genuinely create different goals.
Behavior is the first criterion; lenses then test when relevant distinctions
appear, persist, become inaccessible to a readout, or predict later use beyond
tractable transcript baselines. This is not a claim of information absent from
the complete deterministic model input.

## Research That Changes The Controls

- [Hanna and Mueller, NAACL 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.naacl-long.164/)
  study garden-path mechanisms with SAE features and causal analyses, reporting
  evidence compatible with multiple parses but little reuse of those features
  for follow-up comprehension. Representation, recognition and downstream use
  are separate questions; this is not evidence that our J/R labels encode the
  same structures.
- [AmbigQA](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2004.10645) pairs plausible answers with
  disambiguating question rewrites. Its useful borrowing is a witness connecting
  interpretation to consequences, not an assumption of exhaustive annotation or
  applicability of factoid answers to relational understanding.
- [CLAMBER, ACL 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.578/) distinguishes
  types of ambiguity and evaluates clarification. Its categories can inform
  screening, but cannot settle our construct or make one annotated clarification
  the only useful next conversational move.
- [ImplicatureX, July 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25094v2) studies
  recognition and cancellation, with context/utterance removal and explicit versus
  implicit update controls. Some recognition scores survive removal of the
  evidence: a crucial shortcut control for our corpus. The paper uses elicited
  output judgments as belief proxies, not activation-grounded beliefs; its
  limitations acknowledge disagreement and the need to test actual continuation
  consistency. Its supplied context is modeled as fixed, unlike evolving goals.
- [Beyond Local Surprise, August 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26035v1)
  compares learned grounding controllers over frozen CLIP features on PhotoChat.
  It is not a study of native Qwen belief mechanisms. It motivates separating
  mismatch from revision, but the uncertainty-sensitive controller does not
  clearly beat fixed/pure accumulation on retrieval; the cooperative dataset
  has relatively few strong repair events. Do not generalize its learned
  preserve/revise rule into a universal prescription for our models.

The resulting target is **preserving and revising a usable possibility space**,
including the source/status of its contents, rather than maximizing either
stability or movement of an interpretation.
