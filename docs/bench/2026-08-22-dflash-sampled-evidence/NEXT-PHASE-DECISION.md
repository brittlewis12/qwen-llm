# DFlash Sampled Next-Phase Decision - 2026-08-23

Status: prospective problem frame and recommendation after the final v7
development E0 row. This document grants no measurement, implementation,
held-out, serving, or product authority. The existing preregistration remains
controlling until a separately committed append-only amendment freezes any
changed sequence, command, artifact, and decision rule.

## Decision

- **GO:** prepare an append-only feasibility amendment and seal exact evidence
  scope. Only after that amendment is committed may F0 run. These are the only
  immediate sequential steps.
- **PARALLEL GO:** freeze and test probability/causal K0-S semantics if that work
  does not consume the verifier timebox. Do not start E1b, local sampled-q replay,
  or sparse-q product code.
- **CONDITIONAL GO, effective only after the amendment and F0 are frozen:** run
  one time-boxed, bench-only exact-verifier primitive experiment selected by F0
  for the Q4 target. A microprimitive pass permits only a separately frozen
  causal-block gate, not a full verifier by implication.
- **DEFER:** authoritative E0/E1a/K0/E1b acquisition until the applicable
  verifier shape and all lane policies are frozen. Development observations must
  not enter authoritative reductions.
- **NO-GO:** no serve wiring, sampled product decoder, default change, hidden
  runtime switch, second optimization candidate, or claim that packed target
  rows are serial sampler-v1 p.

## Problem Frame

Positive-temperature speculative decoding has four contracts that must not be
collapsed:

1. **Target p:** the distribution prepared by sampler-v1 from an ordinary
   token-major target row at the exact causal prefix.
2. **Proposal q:** either the deterministic one-hot DFlash2 proposal or the
   selector's causal sparse distribution over its top-16 candidates.
3. **Coupling:** the acceptance/correction algorithm and every proposal,
   acceptance, correction, and target RNG transition.
4. **Verifier/state economics:** how multiple causal target rows are evaluated,
   checkpointed, rolled back, and continued without changing p.

The current layer-major packed verifier supplies a useful different-distribution
diagnostic, not p. Quantized mat-mat and packed reductions can change full logits;
a greedy margin guard cannot make those rows exact for positive-temperature
sampling. Routine token-major replay is exact but cannot be disguised as an
accelerated verifier. The central question is therefore not whether DFlash2 can
coexist with sampling--the one-hot oracle and E0 development sentinels show that
it can--but whether this backend can share enough target work while preserving
serial row arithmetic and state.

The two proposal lanes remain separate:

- **One-hot lane:** `q = delta_y` for the current deterministic DFlash2 token.
  Its exact oracle always emits the one sampler-v1 target draw. It needs E0/E1a
  and economics, not K0.
- **Sparse-q lane:** q is the DFlash2 selector distribution. It needs K0, a
  separately preregistered maximal-coupling E1b, and its own incremental
  economics. A one-hot pass cannot authorize it.

## Evidence At This Boundary

- Post-incident Q4 E0 development rows passed strict serial-versus-capture
  lockstep on the frozen narrow fixtures. V7 added two prompt transitions and
  three generated transitions, then prohibited further development E0
  acquisition in this phase. This is capture-path evidence, not held-out E0
  closure or verifier evidence.
- The E1a development oracle is a strong local differential test of block,
  rejection-carry, state, stop, and continuation integration. Its one-hot
  coupling identity is elementary, it shares substantial target implementation
  lineage with its reference, and it deliberately does not exercise a fast
  verifier. It is neither worthless nor verifier feasibility evidence.
- Q4 drafter exploration found no systematic acceptance collapse in four small
  cells. Q4 is 1.06 GiB versus 1.92 GiB for Q8_0 and loaded faster, but the sample
  cannot select a winner. Q4 remains a prospective arm, not a default.
- Existing packed-target measurements establish a performance opportunity but
  also observed full-logit differences and a near-tie greedy flip. They cannot
  be used as exact p or priced as if already solved.
- Upstream research corrects an earlier concern: DFlash2 does define
  positive-temperature sparse q. The unresolved work is exact local realization
  and cross-implementation semantics, not invention of the distribution.

## Why Amend The Original Sequence

The original preregistration requires G1 economics with an allowance for an
exact verifier, then permits one verifier primitive spike. That controls
optimization selection, but it leaves the dominant engineering premise
unmeasured: an allowance is a maximum affordable cost, not evidence that a
row-stable exact verifier can meet it.

Acquiring a large authoritative packet first would spend heavily on an oracle
whose acceleration mechanism may be structurally infeasible. Building a full
verifier first would risk open-ended optimization for a lane later killed by
acceptance. A cheap but unrepresentative kernel could also create false
confidence. The Pareto compromise is staged uncertainty reduction:

1. seal exact evidence scope and contamination status before more development;
2. freeze the sequencing amendment without acquiring held-out data;
3. derive conservative per-target budgets and rank falsifiers by expected
   downstream cost avoided per engineering day;
4. test one economically material exact primitive under a hard effort budget;
5. only on success, freeze and test one causal verification block;
6. only on causal-block success, freeze and test the full target-only verifier;
7. only on target-specific full-verifier success, acquire authoritative lane
   evidence.

This amendment would supersede only the ordering of the feasibility spike. It
would not reinterpret prior rows, weaken G1, expose held-out fixtures, or grant
product authority.

## L0 - Scope And Evidence Seal

Before F0 measurement or verifier code, the amendment must:

- hash exact held-out prompt bytes/tokens, request lengths, roles, seeds, model
  assets, and an untouched reserve set without running them;
- record every known prior prompt/fixture exposure and prevent exposed
  development rows from entering held-out or reserve reductions;
- freeze separate target/drafter/sampler/context matrices and state exactly which
  cells must pass--a favorable cell cannot open a different target or regime;
- freeze paired run order, warm-state and thermal handling, timing estimators and
  confidence bounds, memory limits, invalid-run rules, and reducers;
- keep held-out content unavailable to primitive selection and repair.

The archetype names and historical seeds are already public. L0 does not pretend
they are secret; it prevents later choice of exact bytes, roles, or reserves from
responding to F0/V0/V0.5/V1 outcomes.

Before fixing cluster count, simulate or otherwise document the frozen interval
estimator's coverage and power at the decision boundaries. If four seeds per
archetype cannot support the declared confidence claim, increase the prospective
count before acquisition. The reserve set is confirmatory only: held-out or
reserve results may stop a lane but may not repair policy, select a primitive, or
change the other set's reducer.

## F0 - Analytical And Value-Of-Information Screen

F0 must use already classified development measurements, a prospectively frozen
calibration set distinct from held-out/reserve evidence, or an explicitly
declared sensitivity grid. Each target gets a separate F0. Within a target, F0
must report S1 and S2, context bands, and Q4/Q8 drafter arms separately. No
pooled acceptance or held-out data may select the primitive.

Q4 target is the first portfolio constraint because it owns the completed
development E0 rows and is the requested starting arm; F0 ranks Q4 primitives,
not Q4 versus Q8. Q8 remains a separate future F0/V0/V0.5/V1 decision and is not
opened by Q4 success or killed by Q4 failure.

For each cell define:

```text
B_1.15 = (Q_low * T_serial_low / 1.15) - T_non_verifier_high
```

`Q_low`, `T_serial_low`, and `T_non_verifier_high` must be joint conservative
bounds under the frozen estimator, not favorable point substitutions.
`T_non_verifier_high` charges draft, capture, q work where applicable, sampling,
correction/state work, command boundaries, synchronization/readback, rollback,
continuation, allocation/buffer initialization, and memory traffic. `B_1.15` is
the maximum affordable verifier time under the stricter 1.15x point target. It
is not a speedup claim.

Decompose the verifier allowance across quantized linears/output projection,
attention, recurrent GDN/conv, state publication/checkpoints, synchronization,
and sampler readback. For each candidate falsifier, record its physical/dispatch
floor, share of the full cost/risk, estimated engineering effort, and downstream
work avoided on failure. Select exactly one V0 before code. An output-projection
experiment is eligible only if this decomposition shows it is economically
material and representative enough to change the V1 decision.

Freeze the VOI scoring inputs and tie rule before selection. At minimum report
ranges for failure probability, downstream engineering cost avoided, and V0
effort, with `VOI = P_fail * cost_avoided / effort`. Select only a candidate that
remains preferred under the frozen adverse ranges; if none does, F0 is
`inconclusive` and grants no V0 authority.

For the selected target/cell, let `V_floor` be the defended full-V1 floor implied
by the component screen. Enter V0 only where:

```text
B_1.15 > 0
V_floor <= 0.80 * B_1.15
```

This is equivalent to B being at least 25% above the floor and leaves 20% of B
unused. The amendment must use that convention consistently. Kill before coding
if the floor cannot be defended, any required target cell fails the reserve,
timing uncertainty dominates without a frozen calibration path, or the result
depends on pricing current packed different-distribution p as exact.

Here, "any required target cell" means every required cell for the selected Q4
target matrix. It does not pool with or decide the separately gated Q8 target.

## V0 - One F0-Selected Critical Primitive

V0 is target-only and proposal-independent at its input boundary. F0 chooses one
economically material exactness premise--for example row-stable quantized weight
reuse, causal attention-frontier integration, or recurrent-state publication--
rather than automatically choosing the easiest kernel.

- freeze exact inputs, `N = 1, 2, 4, 8` where applicable, oracle outputs/state,
  absolute exactness and cost thresholds, peak/transient memory, and commands;
- preserve the relevant serial arithmetic/order and compare every material bit
  against the ordinary target primitive;
- compare current packed behavior only as a labeled non-exact performance
  ceiling;
- report paired wall/GPU time, dispatches, traffic/bandwidth, scratch,
  synchronization, and the component's bounded contribution to full V1 cost;
- if output projection is selected, feed identical post-final-norm rows to both
  arms, compare every vocabulary-logit bit, then replay sampler-v1 as an output-
  plumbing check. That replay adds no evidence about earlier causal layers.

V0 has one prospectively frozen engineering budget, one primitive, one bench/test
entrypoint, and no drafter, proposal, acceptance, generation, serve, or policy
wiring. It does not prove full-verifier feasibility.

An exactness mismatch kills the arithmetic premise. A frozen cost/memory miss
kills only the measured target/shape. Infrastructure failure is invalid before
observation. Exhausting the engineering budget is `budget_no_go` or
`inconclusive`, not proof of structural impossibility. Any second premise or
automatic optimization iteration requires a new gate. Q4 failure does not
automatically decide Q8, and Q4 success grants no Q8 authority.

## V0.5 - One Causal Verification Block

V0 success permits only a separately frozen V0.5. It must exercise the selected
primitive in one complete causal target verification packet/block--not merely
one model layer--at the intended packet width, with private state, exact
prompt/carry inputs, intermediate checkpoints, partial acceptance restore, and
continuation. Include one short sentinel and one dissimilar or long-context
guardrail. Require exact material outputs/state, prohibit routine whole-row
token-major fallback, and require measured adverse whole-packet cost
`<= 0.80 * B_1.15` for the applicable regime on both sentinels. Publish a
coverage map showing that every economically material V1 component uses the
selected premise or a pre-existing exact mechanism; needing another optimization
premise is a V0.5 no-go. V0.5 failure stops V1.

## V1 - Full Target-Only Exact Verifier

V0.5 success permits only a target-specific preregistered V1. V1 must extend the
same row-stable premise through a complete target verification block while
keeping causal operations token-ordered:

- attention sees only each row's exact KV frontier;
- GDN and convolution recurrent state advance in token order;
- requested DFlash target hidden rows have the exact capture semantics;
- every prefix has an exact checkpoint for partial acceptance/rollback;
- full logits or complete sampler-v1 distributions match ordinary token-major
  rows bit-for-bit;
- accepted-prefix restore, pending token, stop boundary, and one-token
  continuation match exactly;
- no margin heuristic or routine token-major fallback is allowed;
- paired whole-verifier measurements and the component sum must agree within a
  frozen tolerance;
- measured adverse-bound verifier cost must satisfy
  `V1_measured_high <= 0.80 * B_1.15` in every required target regime.

Kill V1 on any exactness/state/continuation mismatch, fallback, memory bound
violation, cost failure, or architecture expansion beyond the one frozen
premise. Q4 and Q8 receive separate V1 decisions. A V1 pass is feasibility
evidence only; it does not authorize an integrated sampled decoder or product
integration.

## K0-S And K0-L - Sparse-q Contract

The authoritative semantic references are now identifiable:

- released Q4 asset revision
  `6cb5872e2cee6b4e780a8414922350be8e42d65c`, local SHA-256
  `18a380efc9b7ed8d88677fc895f5c11ae170653434ee378f7348f715c14d0594`;
- author-maintained MLX reference `z-lab/dflash` commit
  `07ebd93db9f472af339b644bb70221ad8428328a`:
  `https://github.com/z-lab/dflash/tree/07ebd93db9f472af339b644bb70221ad8428328a`;
- merged vLLM reference commit
  `b389ac29465b33f9e9c534df221ea3c129e9793f`:
  `https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/tree/b389ac29465b33f9e9c534df221ea3c129e9793f`;
- same-GGUF llama.cpp PR 27342 head
  `1deefcca395743049c3820ab8f9b15043f3e9446`, still open when checked:
  `https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/pull/27342`.

For candidate `b`, predecessor `a`, and projected hidden row `h_t`, upstream
DFlash2 defines:

```text
score_t(a, b) = unary_t(b) + dot(A(a) * H(h_t), B(b))
q_t(b | a) = softmax(score_t(a, b) / request_temperature)
```

Raw selector q is normalized over the backend-selected set of up to 16 candidate
token IDs and is zero elsewhere. Candidate slot order, top-k ties, and duplicate
behavior are backend-specific replay inputs; no portable ordering is assumed.
Target top-k/top-p/min-p and other target processors do not apply to raw q. The
selected token becomes the next causal predecessor. An independently tunable
proposal temperature, epsilon mass, duplicate repair, target filtering, or
silent nonfinite drop would be a new local policy, not upstream parity.

If backend abstention is enabled, define a separate operational kernel over the
candidate IDs plus `ABSTAIN`. The pinned llama.cpp policy first samples raw q and
maps a draw with `q(token) < p_min` to `ABSTAIN`; the surviving emitted-token
distribution must not be described as unchanged q. It also clears the accumulated
proposal block and distributions when the surviving block is shorter than its
configured `n_min`, so operational abstention is block/history dependent.
`p_min`, `n_min`, clearing, and their costs are separately frozen policy/economic
decisions.

There is no universal upstream RNG stream: MLX uses categorical sampling, vLLM
uses stateless candidate-keyed Gumbel-max, and the pinned llama.cpp PR uses a
domain-shifted `std::mt19937` plus `std::discrete_distribution`. K0 must therefore
separate:

1. cross-implementation semantic/causal parity for support, predecessor chain,
   score formula, request-temperature normalization, and issue handling;
2. exact local bit/RNG replay under one prospectively selected, request-local,
   domain-separated RNG policy, explicitly labeled a new local realization;
3. backend-specific sampled-stream replay only for the named backend, with no
   claim that streams are portable across MLX, vLLM, llama.cpp, and this repo.

K0-S freezes and checks support, causal predecessor, score formula/comparator,
request-temperature normalization, and issue behavior without selecting a local
RNG from acceptance outcomes. It may run in parallel with F0 if separately
resourced. Before K0-L, freeze candidate ordering/ties, score precision,
normalization arithmetic, q RNG, acceptance RNG, correction RNG, optional
abstention, and failure behavior. Trace every ordered candidate ID, unary/final
score bit pattern, predecessor choice, issue, q weight, RNG pre/draw/post state,
and selected token. Fail on unexplained support, predecessor, score/comparator,
normalization, RNG, duplicate, sentinel, or nonfinite behavior. No observed row
may be repaired.

K0-S is a **GO for probability/causal contract work**. K0-L and E1b remain
**NO-GO** until the new local RNG policy is frozen and sparse-q economics retains
headroom. K0 remains independent of one-hot E1a.

## Authoritative Lane Entry

Only after the applicable target-specific V1 freezes a feasible exact target
verifier should an authoritative packet acquire L0-sealed held-out request/seed
clusters. Verify that target/drafter arms, S1/S2, fixtures, seeds, q/RNG policy,
verifier shape, commands, reducers, costs, and abstention remain identical to
their frozen identities before acquisition.

- **One-hot:** absolute E0 and E1a correctness, then charged economics.
- **Sparse q:** absolute E0, K0-S, the applicable frozen K0-L replay, and
  separately specified E1b coupling correctness, then charged economics
  including q normalization/RNG/coupling.
- **Both lanes:** one-sided request-cluster 95% LCB at least 1.10x on both
  promotion archetypes, median at least 1.15x, and no important cluster below
  0.98x unless a development-frozen abstention excludes it before speculative
  cost.

The frozen maximal-coupling derivation must establish algebraically that its
emitted marginal is p for the defined finite p and q. E1b then tests event-level
implementation conformance and exact replay against an independent oracle;
finite sampled streams are not proof of the marginal law. Unlike the special
one-hot construction, E1b must not promise token-for-token identity with a plain
serial run under an unrelated RNG mapping.

These gates authorize only lane evidence and projected economics. They do not
establish correctness of an integrated fast sampled decoder. Any product phase
must separately preregister and pass an end-to-end differential gate composing
the V1 verifier, proposal implementation, coupling, RNG domains, rollback, stop,
and continuation paths.

Failure at any gate stops only the applicable target/proposal lane. Reopening
requires a named new premise and a new append-only preregistration, not an
optimization loop over unfavorable evidence.

## Generalization Beyond DFlash2

A successful target-side primitive may be reusable for MTP, autoregressive
external drafters, prompt lookup, and future tree proposals, but operational
support is unproven until each packet width/state/rollback shape passes its own
gate. Each method still needs its own q provenance, causal proposal state,
coupling, acceptance, rollback, and economics. DFlash selector-lattice K0,
DFlash hidden capture semantics, quantization-specific acceptance, and Qwen
KV/GDN/conv state remain isolated and must not be generalized.

The durable rule is to label p, q, verifier distribution, RNG domains, causal
state, and charged costs separately. A proposal failure must not condemn the
target primitive; a target exactness/economic failure must not be hidden by a
better proposal.

## Adversarial Review Resolution

The first hostile review found the v7 result sound but challenged the original
verifier-after-G1 order as circular. Independent sequencing and technical
reviews favored staged falsification. A second red team rejected automatic
lm-head-first V0 as a cheap-proxy trap and identified late scope sealing, weak
timing definitions, and the V0-to-V1 jump. The revised ladder therefore seals L0
first, makes F0 select the highest-value critical primitive, distinguishes
engineering-budget exhaustion from technical failure, and inserts causal V0.5
before target-specific V1.

The sparse-q review also overturned the hypothesis that sampled selector q would
necessarily be a new local invention. Upstream defines the distribution, but
not one portable RNG realization. The recommendation therefore advances K0
contract work while keeping E1b and product sampling blocked.
