quality verdict. Vendor guidance differs from the retired pilot's settings:

quality proof. The historical 17/32 (second review 16/32) versus Maya 22/32 pilot

TokenLimit; selected effort-none followups avoided truncation but retained answer
defects, and doubled low-effort budgets did not establish a remedy. Selected
followups are not a replacement eight-case score.
