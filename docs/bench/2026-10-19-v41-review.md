## Ranked Performance Leads

1. Measure current-tree attention stages, then extend shared matrix machinery
   before adding another family-specific kernel. The current F32 value-matrix
   call selects scalar grouped arithmetic; the generic MMA eligibility in
   `metal/latent.rs` is Q6_K-only. Multi-query attention is another candidate,
   but a 256-row chunk exceeds the 128-row raw ring: blindly writing all keys
   first would destroy history needed by early queries.
2. Measure routed-expert occupancy. With six routes and 272 experts, 256 prompt
   rows average 5.65 assignments per expert, versus 16 gate/up and 32 down tile
   slots. Actual skew, occupied tiles and weight traffic must decide between
   smaller tiles and larger memory-admitted chunks.
3. Measure residual per-row projected RMS and per-group output packing/calls.
   These are lower-risk sharing opportunities, not evidence of a 4.2x saving.

The older 1K profile attributed 9.84 GPU seconds to attention and 3.29 to FFN.
It predates the last optimizations and cannot attribute the final checkpoint.
Mixed precision is a separate arithmetic-contract decision, not a silent way
to reuse F16 primitives under a NativeF32 label.
