# V4.1 prefill chunk 1024

Candidate `69318883` raises `deepseek_v41::forward::DEFAULT_CHUNK_ROWS` from
256 to 1024. The grouped expert tile is 16 slots wide; with 384 experts and
top-6, 1024 rows average 16 slots per expert, exactly filling one tile. At 256
rows the average bucket uses a quarter of a tile. The CLI allocator already
prices 1024/2048, so only the model's hardcoded chunk changed.

## M5 ABBA

- M5 Ultra, Metal compiler `32023.921`, SDK 27.0, validation off, production
  lease, one fresh model load per arm, no competing benchmark.
- Baseline `4b1fca04` (`~/code/qwen-llm-v41-base`, chunk 256), candidate
  `69318883` (`~/code/qwen-llm-fat-m5`, chunk 1024), both `build-info` status
  `match`.
- Q2_K_XL and IQ3_XXS, `qwen-bench suite --pp 1024 --runs 3 --seed 1`, arm
  order base/candidate/candidate/base, unique per-arm files. The candidate row
  reports `prefill_chunk=1024`; the baseline row reports 256.

| Quant | base 1 | cand 2 | cand 3 | base 4 | base mean | cand mean | delta |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Q2 | 179.598 | 204.131 | 204.189 | 179.479 | 179.539 | 204.160 | **+13.71%** |
| IQ3 | 204.506 | 257.975 | 257.996 | 205.088 | 204.797 | 257.986 | **+25.97%** |

Within-arm SD is 0.07-0.58 tok/s; both candidate samples sit above both
baseline samples on each artifact. The box was otherwise idle.

## Interpretation

The gain tracks how much of each artifact is already on the F32 grouped tile.
IQ3 groups 35/40 layers and gains 1.26x. Q2 groups 25/40; the other 15 layers
(all IQ2_XXS, plus two IQ2_XS) loop native rows and do not amortize, so Q2
gains 1.14x. This confirms the chunk lever is tile fill, not dispatch overhead.

The next Q2 lever is grouped F32 gate/up/down for IQ2_XXS and IQ2_XS. IQ3's next
lever is attention: it is 42.9% of the accepted PP1024 profile and becomes a
larger share after the expert time shrinks.

PP2048 and the ignored 257-token ring-dispatch witness are not requalified by
this experiment. The witness expectations assume 256-row chunks and must be
updated before the chunk change is treated as production-ready.
