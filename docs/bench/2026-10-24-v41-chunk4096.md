# V4.1 4096-row Full chunk: Q2 win, IQ3 stability guard

Probe on main `1c7c3ef8`, Q2_K_XL pp4096, M5 Ultra 80-core, macOS 27.0.1,
240 GiB MWSS, cached-load fix, score32 default. Control is two 2048-row
chunks; candidate is one 4096-row chunk. Build-vs-build ABBA, two runs per
arm, seed 1. Raw: `metagross:~/qwen-runs/2026-10-24-v41-chunk4096/`.

## Q2 result

| Arm | Samples (tok/s) | Mean |
|---|---:|---:|
| control (2048 chunks) | 847.310, 837.866 | 842.588 |
| candidate (4096 chunk) | 871.596, 871.913 | **871.755** |

**+3.46%** at pp4096. The candidate is stable across both samples; the
control spread is ~1.1%. pp2048 with the same binary is unchanged within
noise (875.3 vs 877.3).

The gain is consistent with one chunk instead of two: the 64-slot dense
tiles see 4096 query rows in one pass, and chunk-boundary/scratch setup
is halved. The expert panels are still 32-slot (two panels per expert at
4096), so this is not the expert-tail lever.

## IQ3 result

| Arm | Samples (tok/s) | Mean |
|---|---:|---:|
| control (2048 chunks) | 838.487, 836.996 | 837.742 |
| candidate (4096 chunk) | 874.732, 827.866 | 851.299 |

The candidate spread is 5.6% and the slower sample is below the control.
IQ3 is not qualified for the 4096 chunk on this evidence; its ~217 GiB
resident plan has less stability margin at the larger scratch envelope.

## Selector

`Forward` chooses the Full chunk from packed+dense resident bytes:

- `<= 200 GiB`: `MAX_CHUNK_ROWS = 4096` (Q2 ~186 GiB, Maya-S ~166 GiB).
- `> 200 GiB`: `DEFAULT_CHUNK_ROWS = 2048` (IQ3 ~217 GiB).

`MAX_CHUNK_ROWS` is the admission bound; `DEFAULT_CHUNK_ROWS` remains the
safe direct-call value. The bench/serve extent uses the 4096 maximum so
the larger scratch is priced when selected.

## Limits

- Build-vs-build ABBA, one Q2 and one IQ3 artifact; no IQ3 win claim.
- The held-text witness packet is 576 tokens, so it does not exercise the
  4096-row chunk; a 4096-token logits comparison is still owed before a
  broader quality/default claim. Per-row arithmetic is unchanged in
  construction, but this is not a measured 4096-token quality result.
- The 200 GiB selector is a heuristic based on the two measured artifacts
  and the resident plans, not a universal fit boundary.
