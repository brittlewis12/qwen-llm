# V4.1 64-slot gate/up panel at 4096: +1.67% model

Candidate `perf/v41-expert-wide64` on main `28d23ac9` (4096-row chunk
selector). M5 Ultra 80-core, macOS 27.0.1, 240 GiB MWSS. Q2_K_XL,
`qwen-bench suite --pp 4096 --runs 2 --seed 1`, same binary; ABBA with
`QWEN_V41_EXPERT_WIDE=0` as the narrow control. Raw:
`metagross:~/qwen-runs/2026-10-24-v41-expert-wide/`.

## Role split

The 64-slot panel is a gate/up win, not a down win. One-layer fixture,
IQ3_XXS, 4096 routed tokens, 16 KiB threadgroup:

| Role | narrow 32-slot | wide 64-slot | change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| down | 11,370.1 us | 16,289.7 us | **+43% slower** |
| gate+up | 30,277.9 us | 23,407.5 us | **−22.7%** |

The wide down tile is not selected. Gate/up is, because its K=5120 loop
amortizes the wider output tile and the extra activation slot group.
Down keeps the narrow tile at both 2048 and 4096 rows.

Correctness at a nonzero 4096-token, N_IN=512 / N_OUT=256 shape is
bitwise identical to the narrow tile for both down and gate/up:
`max|delta| = 0`, `cos = 1.000000`.

## Model throughput

Q2_K_XL pp4096, same binary, gate/up wide selected by default:

| Arm | Samples (tok/s) | Mean |
|---|---:|---:|
| narrow (`QWEN_V41_EXPERT_WIDE=0`) | 877.254, 873.757 | 875.506 |
| wide gate/up (default) | 890.778, 889.565 | **890.172** |

**+1.67%** at pp4096. Q2 pp2048 is unaffected because the 4096-row
selector is not reached. IQ3 stays on the 2048-row chunk path, so the
wide gate/up tile is not selected there.

## Selection

`encode_moe_swiglu_f32_grouped_slots_tensor` selects the 64-slot gate/up
tile when all of the following hold:

- `n_tokens >= 4096` (the Full chunk selector has already chosen 4096 for
  fitting artifacts),
- the clamped epilogue is requested,
- the tensor tier is selected,
- the gate and up dtypes have wide instantiations,
- `QWEN_V41_EXPERT_WIDE` is not `0`.

The down encoder is unchanged. The wide kernel is instantiated for every
generic expert dtype.

## Limits

- One Q2 artifact, one machine, one layer fixture. The 576-token
  held-text witness does not exercise the 4096 path; a 4096-token logits
  comparison is still owed before a model-wide quality claim.
- The bitwise equivalence test covers IQ3_XXS; other dtypes share the
  same template and dequant function but were not individually exercised
  in the model.
- The 4096 chunk selector is a prerequisite; IQ3 remains at 2048 for
  stability.
