# V4.1 attention: wider 32-key tensor score, +2.8%

Candidate `perf/v41-score32-land` on `d0ea399a` (the cached-load fix).
M5 Ultra 80-core, macOS 27.0.1, 240 GiB wired limit, production lease.
Same-binary ABCCBA with `QWEN_V41_SINK_TENSOR32=0` as the 16-key tensor
control. Raw: `metagross:~/qwen-runs/2026-10-24-v41-score32-fixed/`.

## What changed

The tensor score tile now processes **32 keys per panel**, splitting the
512-wide latent into two 256-wide chunks. Q remains resident in 16 KiB;
the K slab uses the other 16 KiB; the 32×16 F32 score block aliases the
K slab after both matmuls. The threadgroup budget is the same 32 KiB as
the 16-key form. Softmax, tensor PV and normalize are unchanged.

The wider N fixes the main efficiency gap in the sink: the old score
matmul was 16×16×512, while PV already ran 16×512×16. The new score is
16×32×256 twice, with the same staged half operands and F32 sums.

Selected by default on the Metal 4 tensor tier from 128 rows;
`QWEN_V41_SINK_TENSOR32=0` restores the 16-key form.

dispatch; its ~18% reduction is ~13% of the complete sink.

## Limits

- One M5 Ultra, one held-text packet, one Q2 throughput artifact. IQ3
  throughput was not re-measured after this landing; the IQ3 witness is
  the quality evidence.
- The fixture is synthetic and reports GPU command time, not a full-model
  attribution.
- Score is still far below PV; register-cooperative operands, larger
  materialized score GEMMs and all-head/dimension-split designs remain
  open. The half-KV source view adds only ~2% full sink on top of score32
  (probe branch, not landed) and needs persistent-view lifecycle work.
- The return-only expert-grid probe (width 64 vs 2) measured -5.4% of the
  combined expert tile (~1.3% model arithmetic) on the 31/32/33 route
  fixture; no production compact worklist was built.
