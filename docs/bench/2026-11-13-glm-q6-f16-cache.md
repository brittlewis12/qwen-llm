# GLM Q6 F16 prefill cache: rejected at the isolated-stage gate

Status: closed. Candidate source commit `c3afae728`; default-off during the
measurement. The cache is removed from the worktree; this note and the raw
records remain.

## Candidate and cell

The candidate materialized non-expert Q6_K projection weights into one
256-byte-aligned resident F16 arena at model load and selected F16 mat-mat for
Fast packed chunks of at least 512 rows. It used the same Q6_K half-tile
decoder as the quantized matrix tile. Decode and shorter chunks stayed on the
existing path. No weights were requantized.

One screening run used the stock GLM-5.3 Flash UD-IQ3_XXS artifact on M5 Ultra,
`iter`, at depth 49,152 with a 2,048-row chunk and production routing. This
matches the full-chunk regime, whose routed-row mean was 56.9 per expert
(p50 41, p90 125). Pressure stayed at 0. The candidate load admitted three
48K sessions: the 3-session buffer plan was 9,700,638,720 bytes (9.03 GiB),
with `AdmittedWithHostBudget`. Retained model plus cache bytes were
131,122,514,176 (122.12 GiB).

The ordinary wall and stage profile below are screening measurements against
the earlier baseline census, not a paired A/B. The pre-load snapshot had no
other model resident. Background `~/ghost` work included a `shadps4` worker at
158% CPU and `drive.py` at 10%; load average rose from 8.85/13.65/15.66 to
22.82/17.28/16.86. Pressure stayed at 0. The candidate wall was stable across
five samples; stage samples were stable across three.

## Isolated-stage gate

The required stage speedup was at least 1.25x. Every Q6-heavy projection span
was slower or effectively unchanged:

| Stage | Baseline ms | F16-cache ms | Baseline / candidate |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Dense FFN | 24.79 | 65.88 | 0.376x |
| Shared expert | 64.61 | 158.60 | 0.407x |
| KDA input projections | 177.92 | 177.59 | 1.002x |
| KDA output projection | 62.88 | 171.79 | 0.366x |
| MLA projection | 42.88 | 49.33 | 0.869x |
| MLA output projections | 74.44 | 143.07 | 0.520x |

Routed experts remained effectively unchanged at 721.28 ms baseline versus
720.71 ms candidate. Candidate ordinary wall was 2,323.30 ms (samples
2,322.11–2,324.47), against the earlier 1,999.71 ms baseline median. Its
sampled stage-profile wall was 2,535.81 ms, against 1,998.35 ms baseline.
Stage spans are not additive; the candidate span sum exceeded command GPU time
by 83.58 ms.

**Decision: remove the cache candidate.** It misses the isolated-stage gate by
a wide margin, so no paired wall, quality, or serve gate was run. The next
lever is the sorted expert-major fused routed-expert path from V4.1, selected
against the 721 ms routed-expert span and the occupancy profile at 2,048 rows.
